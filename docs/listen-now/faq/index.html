What does Listen Now! do?

It adds an audio player to your Shopify storefront, tied to a custom app block in your theme — no code needed.

Who is it for?

Any Shopify merchant, from one track to thousands. Common uses: podcasts, music samples, audiobooks, customer testimonials, product sounds, and blog narration via text-to-speech.

Can I manage lots of tracks at once?

Yes — see Bulk Upload and Bulk Edit (Starter and Pro plans; Free plan gets a limited version).

Can I group tracks into a playlist?

Yes, see Playlists.

Does it work on product pages?

Yes for a single audio track and for the text-to-speech widget. See Turning On the Player.

What file types and sizes are supported?

MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, and M4A, up to 20 MB when uploading directly in the app (the Upload Track wizard allows up to 50 MB).

What if my theme doesn't support it?

The app needs a theme built on Shopify's modern app-block system (Online Store 2.0). Most current themes qualify. If yours doesn't, the app block won't be available in your Theme Editor.

Can I see how many people played a track?

Yes, on the Starter and Pro plans — see Analytics.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

See My Account.
