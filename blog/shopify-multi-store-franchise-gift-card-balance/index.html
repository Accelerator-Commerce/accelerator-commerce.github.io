Can a Shopify Gift Card Be Used Across Multiple Stores?

Short answer: No, not by default. A Shopify gift card is issued by, and only redeemable at, the specific Shopify store that created it. If you run a franchise, a multi-brand group, or several regional storefronts under one company, a gift card bought at one won't validate or show a balance at another unless you add a third-party sync layer or build custom integration between the stores.

Why merchants assume it should "just work"

To a customer, a gift card usually feels tied to the brand, not to a specific storefront's backend. If a company operates three Shopify stores for three regional markets, or a franchise group runs a separate Shopify instance per location, shoppers reasonably expect a gift card bought at one to work at any of them. Shopify doesn't share gift card records between separate store instances at all — each store is its own isolated database, and a gift card is a record inside that one store's data, not a brand-wide asset.

What merchants have found when they hit this

Several independent Shopify Community threads over the years ask versions of the same question: how to make a gift card work across multiple stores. One merchant running a Shopify Plus multi-store setup asked directly about sharing gift vouchers across storefronts. Another asked how to transfer gift cards between two separate stores after a business restructuring. A third asked about synchronizing gift card balances between two stores in real time. In every case, the answer was the same: Shopify has no native cross-store gift card feature, and merchants had to reach for one of a few workarounds.

What actually works instead

  • A third-party sync layer. Some apps act as a shared "wallet" that sits on top of multiple connected Shopify stores, keeping a gift card's balance in sync across all of them in near real time. This is the closest thing to true cross-store gift cards, but it adds an ongoing app dependency and cost.
  • Custom API-level integration. A developer can build a middleware layer that intercepts gift card redemption and balance-check requests and routes them to whichever store actually holds the record. This gives the most control but requires ongoing maintenance as each store's app stack changes.
  • One-time CSV migration. For a one-off situation — consolidating stores, or migrating a customer base — bulk-import tools can move existing gift card balances from one store into another. This isn't live sync; it's a snapshot migration, useful for a one-time event rather than ongoing multi-store operation.
  • Consolidate to one store. Where the business structure allows it, some multi-location or multi-brand sellers run a single Shopify store with markets, currencies, or a B2B channel handling regional differences, rather than separate store instances — which sidesteps the gift card problem entirely because there's only one store's worth of records.

Franchises specifically

Franchise setups are the sharpest version of this problem, because franchise owners often expect (and are sometimes told to expect, by the franchisor) that a gift card is a brand-wide instrument regardless of which location a customer bought it from or is redeeming it at. If each location runs its own Shopify store rather than being a location within one shared store, that expectation needs a sync layer or explicit franchise-wide gift card policy built on top of Shopify — it isn't something Shopify provides out of the box.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro checks the balance of gift cards issued by the Shopify store it's installed on. It doesn't create cross-store sharing on its own — no app changes the fact that Shopify stores are isolated data instances — but for a single store (or for each store in a multi-store group running its own balance checker), it gives customers and staff a fast, accurate way to see exactly what's left on a card issued there.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shopify have a native setting to share gift cards between stores?

No. Each Shopify store's gift cards are isolated to that store. There's no admin setting or native API that shares gift card balances across separate store instances.

What's the simplest fix for a small multi-location business?

If the locations can operate as one Shopify store (using markets, POS locations, or a B2B channel for regional differences) rather than separate store instances, gift cards work store-wide automatically, since there's only one store's data to begin with.

Is there an app that syncs gift card balances across stores in real time?

Yes — some apps position themselves specifically as a shared balance "wallet" layer across connected Shopify stores. Evaluate them on how quickly balances sync and what happens if one store is temporarily unreachable, since a lag or sync failure can let a card be over-redeemed.
