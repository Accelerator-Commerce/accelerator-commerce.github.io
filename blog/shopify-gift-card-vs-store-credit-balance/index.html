Gift Card or Store Credit? How to Tell Which One You Actually Have

Quick answer: If you have a physical or emailed code you'd type in at checkout, it's a gift card — check its balance with the store's gift card balance checker using that code. If a store credited an amount to your account after a return or refund and there was never a code involved, it's store credit — it should apply automatically when you're logged in and checking out, with no code to enter or look up.

Why this mix-up happens so often

Gift cards and store credit both show up as "money you can spend at this store that isn't cash," which is exactly why shoppers and even store staff mix them up. But they're built differently under the hood, and that difference determines where you actually go to check what's left on either one. Getting it backwards is the single most common reason someone searches for a "gift card balance" and comes up empty — they're actually holding store credit, or vice versa.

The core difference

  • A gift card is a product with a code. It's purchased (by you or someone buying it for you), it has its own unique code or card number, and it can usually be transferred — you can hand a gift card code to someone else and they can spend it. It exists independently of any customer account.
  • Store credit is a balance tied to your account. It's typically issued by the store itself, most often after a return or refund, and it's attached directly to your customer profile rather than to a separate code. It generally isn't transferable — it's yours, tied to your login.

How to tell which one you actually have

  • Do you have a code, card number, or PIN? If yes, it's almost certainly a gift card. Store credit doesn't come with a code to redeem — it's just a number that sits on your account.
  • Did it come from a return or refund? Store credit is the most common outcome of a "credit instead of a refund to your original payment method" return. If that's how you got it, you're very likely looking at store credit, not a gift card.
  • Was it a purchase, a gift, or a reward? Something someone bought and sent to you (or that you bought for yourself) is a gift card. A balance that appeared on your account automatically, with no purchase step, is more likely store credit or a loyalty reward.

Where to actually check the balance for each

This is where the confusion turns into a real support problem: the two are checked in different places, and using the wrong one will always come up empty even if the balance is real.

  • Gift card balance: use the store's dedicated gift card balance checker (or the store's own gift card lookup page) and enter the code or card number printed on it.
  • Store credit balance: log in to your customer account on the store and check your account page directly — there's no code to enter, because the balance isn't attached to a code, it's attached to you. At checkout, an available store credit balance typically applies automatically once you're signed in, without a field to type anything into.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro is built specifically for the gift-card side of this: a shopper enters the code from their card and gets back the remaining balance, without needing an account or a support ticket. If what you're actually holding is store credit rather than a gift card — no code, just a balance that showed up after a return — the right place to check is your account page on the store, not a gift card lookup tool, since the two systems track balances separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can store credit and a gift card balance ever be combined into one number?

Not usually — most Shopify stores track them as two separate balances even if both are usable at checkout, since gift cards use a redemption code and store credit is tied to the account. Don't assume one figure includes the other without checking both.

Why did my gift card code say "invalid" when I know I have a balance?

The most common reason is that there's no code to check in the first place because what you have is actually store credit, not a gift card — try your account page instead of the gift card checker.

Does store credit expire the same way gift cards do?

It depends on the store and local regulations for each — some stores set different expiration or no-expiration rules for store credit versus gift cards. Check the specific store's gift card and return policy rather than assuming the two follow identical rules.
