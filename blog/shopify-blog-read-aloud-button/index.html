Does Shopify Have a "Read Aloud" Button for Blog Posts? How to Add One (Like Medium)

Quick answer: No — Shopify has no built-in "listen to this article" button. The listen feature people associate with Medium is that platform's own product, not something Shopify ships with. You can add an equivalent read-aloud button to a Shopify blog with an app like Listen Now.

The comparison merchants keep making

A merchant on the Shopify Community described exactly what they wanted: something like Medium's listen button, sitting right at the top of the article, so a reader could tap it and hear the post instead of scrolling through it. They weren't asking about "text-to-speech" in the abstract — they were asking for a specific, familiar interaction, because they'd used it themselves as a Medium reader and wanted their own Shopify blog to feel the same way.

That's a useful way to frame the request, because most people already know what Medium's button feels like: a small control near the headline, a play icon, a narrator's voice that starts on tap. Shopify blogs don't come with anything like it out of the box — the theme renders your article as text, full stop. Getting the same experience means adding it.

What a "read aloud" button actually needs to do well

Not every audio add-on delivers the experience merchants have in mind when they picture Medium's button. A few things separate a read-aloud button that works from one that's just technically present:

  • It has to be visible. A listen option buried in a menu or a theme setting nobody sees defeats the purpose — readers need to notice it near the top of the post, the same way they'd notice Medium's.
  • It has to work on mobile. A large share of blog traffic is phones, and a button that only behaves on desktop isn't solving the problem for most readers.
  • It shouldn't replace the text. The written article stays on the page. The button adds a second way to consume it — for multitaskers, for people who prefer listening, for accessibility — it doesn't substitute for readable content search engines and screen readers already rely on.
  • The voice has to sound natural enough to finish. A robotic, hard-to-follow narration gets abandoned after a few seconds. The bar isn't perfection, but it needs to be comfortable to listen to for the length of a real article.

Adding a read-aloud button with Listen Now

Listen Now adds a listen control to Shopify blog posts without requiring theme code changes. The setup is a few steps:

  1. Install Listen Now from the Shopify App Store and complete the app's onboarding.
  2. Turn on narration for your blog so posts get a listen button automatically rather than one at a time.
  3. Check placement — the button should sit somewhere a reader lands on naturally, near the top of the article, the way Medium's does.
  4. Test on a phone. Open a live post as a mobile visitor would, tap play, and confirm playback starts cleanly and controls are easy to reach with a thumb.

Once it's live, the button behaves the way the Community request described: a visible, one-tap way to hear the article, without turning the blog post itself into anything other than a normal, readable page.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same thing as "text-to-speech"?

Mechanically, yes — a read-aloud button is a text-to-speech feature. The distinction merchants care about is the experience: a visible, single-tap listen control on the article itself, not a generic audio feature buried somewhere else on the site.

Will adding a read-aloud button change how my blog post looks?

No. The article stays exactly as written — headings, images, links and all. The button adds a small control; it doesn't restyle or shorten the content underneath it.

Does the button work the same way on every device?

It should. If you set it up and only ever check it on desktop, test it on a phone too — that's the device most readers will actually use it from.
