My Shopify Gift Card Never Arrived: Fixing Email and SMS Delivery Problems

Quick answer: "My gift card never arrived" is a delivery problem, not a balance problem — the card was almost always issued correctly, it just didn't reach an inbox or phone. Start by checking spam/promotions folders and the exact email or phone number on the order, then check the store's notification settings and, for older themes, whether direct-to-recipient sending is actually supported before assuming the card doesn't exist.

Don't confuse "never arrived" with "no balance"

These are two different problems that get searched for with nearly identical language. A missing gift card usually means the card was generated by Shopify at the time of purchase and does have a real balance — it just never reached the person who was supposed to receive it, because of an email delivery failure, a wrong recipient detail, or an SMS notification issue. That's worth ruling out first, because it changes where you should be looking for the fix: at delivery settings, not at a balance checker.

Common causes of a gift card email not arriving

  • It landed in spam or promotions. Automated transactional emails, including gift card delivery emails, are exactly the kind of message spam filters sometimes catch — check spam, junk, and promotions tabs before assuming it wasn't sent.
  • The recipient email was mistyped at checkout. If the gift card was purchased for someone else, a typo in the recipient's email address at checkout sends it to an address that doesn't exist or belongs to someone else, with no bounce notice reaching the purchaser.
  • The order was placed as a guest with an unverified email. Some delivery issues trace back to the email provider on the receiving end silently rejecting the message rather than an actual Shopify error.
  • Notification templates were customized incorrectly. If a store has edited its gift card email notification template, a broken template can cause the email to fail to send or render incorrectly. This is a store-side setting worth checking if multiple customers report the same issue.

Why SMS gift card delivery is a separate, less reliable path

SMS delivery of gift cards depends on additional configuration beyond what email delivery needs — a connected SMS provider, correctly formatted phone numbers, and carrier-side delivery, any of which can fail independently of the order itself processing correctly. If a customer expected a text message with their gift card and nothing arrived, check that SMS notifications are actually enabled and configured for gift cards specifically, since it's a separate setting from email notifications rather than a fallback that kicks in automatically.

Direct-to-recipient sending doesn't work the same way on every theme

Shopify has added native support for sending a purchased gift card directly to a recipient's email rather than the purchaser's, but whether that option actually appears at checkout depends on the theme and checkout configuration in use. Older themes or heavily customized checkouts may not expose this option at all, which leads to gift cards defaulting to the purchaser's email with no obvious way to redirect delivery — something worth confirming on your own store's checkout before assuming a customer did something wrong.

What to check, in order

  • Spam, junk, and promotions folders on the exact email address used at checkout.
  • The recipient email or phone number on the order itself, for typos.
  • Whether the store's gift card notification template was customized and might be broken.
  • Whether SMS notifications are separately enabled, if SMS delivery was expected.
  • Whether the theme's checkout actually supports direct-to-recipient sending, if the card was meant to go to someone other than the purchaser.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Once a gift card code does exist — whether it arrived by email, SMS, or was read off a physical card — Gift Check Pro lets anyone confirm its balance directly, which is a useful way to verify a card is real and funded even before a delivery issue gets fully resolved. It's a balance-checking tool rather than a delivery or notification system, so it complements fixing an email or SMS delivery problem rather than replacing that troubleshooting step.

Frequently asked questions

If the gift card email never arrived, does the card still have a balance?

In most cases yes — the card is typically generated and funded at the time of purchase regardless of whether the notification email successfully sends, so a delivery failure doesn't usually mean the balance is missing too. If you have the code from the order confirmation or admin, you can check its balance directly.

Can a store manually resend a gift card email?

Yes — Shopify stores can typically resend order and gift card notifications manually from the order or gift card admin page, which is often faster than troubleshooting why the original send failed.

Is SMS gift card delivery reliable enough to rely on by itself?

Treat SMS as a secondary channel rather than the only one — carrier-side delivery issues and formatting problems are common enough that pairing SMS with an email confirmation is a safer default for anything time-sensitive, like a gift purchased for a specific occasion.
