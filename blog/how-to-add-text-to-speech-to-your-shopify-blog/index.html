How to Add Text-to-Speech to Your Shopify Blog

Quick answer: Install Listen Now from the Shopify App Store, connect it to the content you want to make listenable, configure the player, and test the experience on desktop and mobile.

Why add audio to a Shopify blog?

Blog readers do not always want to read every article on a screen. A listenable version gives customers another way to consume educational content, product stories, guides and announcements.

1. Install Listen Now

Start by installing Listen Now from the Shopify App Store. Follow the app's current onboarding flow and grant the permissions requested by Shopify.

2. Configure your audio experience

Choose the content you want to make available as audio and configure the player to fit your storefront. The goal is to make the listening option obvious without overwhelming the page.

3. Test a real blog post

Open a published article as a customer would. Check the player on desktop and mobile, confirm that the spoken content matches the article, and make sure visitors can start and stop playback easily.

4. Treat audio as an accessibility feature

Audio can be useful for people who prefer listening, but it should complement—not replace—the written article. Keep headings, lists and text available in the page itself.

Frequently asked questions

Will text-to-speech replace my Shopify blog content?

No. Your written article should remain the primary content. Audio is an alternative way to consume it.

Is text-to-speech an SEO trick?

No. The strongest SEO benefit comes from creating genuinely useful content. Audio can improve the content experience for some visitors.
