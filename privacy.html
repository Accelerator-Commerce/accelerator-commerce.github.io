Privacy policy

How Accelerator Commerce collects, uses and shares personal information.

Effective date: 10/20/2023

1. Introduction

Accelerator Commerce ("we," "us," or "our") operates Listen Now! (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share personal information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and other contact details.
  • App Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the App, including user interactions, preferences, and settings.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about the device and browser you use to access the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To respond to your requests and provide customer support.
  • To send you updates, offers, and promotional materials.
  • To monitor and analyze usage patterns.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App such as database providers.
  • Law enforcement agencies, government officials, or other third parties when required by law.

5. Your Choices

  • You can access, update, or delete your account information by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of receiving promotional emails.
  • You can disable cookies or adjust your browser settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our information practices. We will notify you of any material changes.
