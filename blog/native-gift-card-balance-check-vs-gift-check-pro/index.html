Shopify's Native Gift Card Balance Check vs. Gift Check Pro

Short answer: Shopify POS added a barcode-scan balance check for staff at checkout. It's genuinely useful, but it only covers one workflow — a staff member, scanning a physical or on-screen barcode, during a POS session. It doesn't give customers a self-serve way to check a balance online, doesn't cover a storefront lookup by code, and doesn't unify native and app-issued gift cards in one place. That's the gap a dedicated app like Gift Check Pro still fills.

What Shopify's native feature actually does

Merchants have recently noticed — some with surprise — that the Shopify POS app now supports scanning a gift card's barcode to see its remaining balance directly, without adding it to a cart or starting a sale. For a staff member standing at a register with a physical card in hand, that's a fast, simple improvement over the old workaround of ringing up a $0.00 test item just to see a balance on screen.

What it doesn't cover

  • No customer self-service. The native check is a POS staff action. A customer checking their own balance from home, or from their phone before walking into the store, has no equivalent on the storefront.
  • Requires a scannable barcode. A customer who only has a gift card code — read over the phone, typed from an email, or from a card with a worn or missing barcode — can't use the scan flow at all.
  • No unified view. Stores that sell both Shopify-native gift cards and cards issued through a separate app don't get a single place to check either kind; the native scan only knows about Shopify's own gift card records.
  • Online-only stores have no register to scan at. The feature lives in POS, so a store without a physical point of sale gets nothing from it.

Where Gift Check Pro still adds value

Gift Check Pro is built around the workflows the native scan doesn't touch: a storefront page where customers check their own balance by entering a code, no barcode or scanner required, and a consistent balance-checking experience whether a store is online-only, POS-only, or both. For merchants who sell gift cards as a meaningful part of their business, that combination — customer self-service plus staff-facing lookup — covers more of the real-world ways people actually ask "how much is left on this?"

Which one do you need?

They're not mutually exclusive. The native POS scan is a nice quality-of-life improvement for staff with a card in hand at a register. Gift Check Pro is for the customer who wants to check a balance without walking into a store or calling someone, and for merchants who want that self-service option available at all — something the native feature was never built to provide.

Frequently asked questions

Does the native POS scan replace the need for a balance-check app?

Only for the specific case of staff scanning a physical card at a register. It doesn't add a customer-facing, code-based balance check anywhere on the storefront.

Can customers use the native feature themselves?

No — it's a POS staff action inside the Shopify POS app, not something exposed to customers on a storefront.
