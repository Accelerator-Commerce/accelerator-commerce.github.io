Holiday Background Music for Shopify: A Simple Setup Checklist for BFCM and Christmas

Quick answer: Turn on seasonal music a few weeks before BFCM, keep playback visitor-controlled rather than autoplaying, swap the playlist as the season changes, test on mobile before traffic ramps up, and rotate it back off (or back to a general playlist) once the holidays end.

Why most "holiday music" advice stops short

Most content about holiday background music for Shopify is really just a list of which apps have a seasonal playlist. That's a starting point, not a plan. Turning on a Christmas playlist is one setting; doing it at the right time, with the right defaults, and turning it back off again is the part that actually gets skipped. This is a checklist for that part.

1. Set a lead time before BFCM

Publishing this in September isn't a coincidence — it's the lead time that matters. Don't wait until Black Friday week to touch your store's audio setup. Give yourself a few weeks before BFCM to turn on a seasonal playlist, listen through it end to end, and fix anything that sounds off, so you're not making audio changes on your highest-traffic days of the year.

2. Never force autoplay

Whatever playlist you choose, playback should start only when a visitor chooses to start it — never automatically on page load. This lines up with Shopify's own accessibility guidance, which warns that unexpected or auto-playing audio can interfere with screen readers and disorient visitors using assistive technology. A visible play control that a shopper taps themselves avoids that problem entirely, holiday season or not.

3. Keep the control visible and easy to find

A holiday playlist a visitor can't find is doing nothing for the mood of the store. Make sure the player is somewhere shoppers will actually notice, that volume or muting is easy to reach, and that stopping playback takes one interaction, not a hunt through a menu.

4. Swap playlists as the season moves

"Holiday" isn't one static playlist from October through January. Plan the swaps: a lighter autumn or early-BFCM playlist, then a dedicated Christmas rotation once December starts, rather than leaving one generic seasonal mix running the whole stretch. Deciding the swap dates now, while there's no pressure, is easier than doing it mid-season.

5. Test on mobile before traffic ramps up

A large share of BFCM traffic arrives on phones. Before the season starts, open the storefront as a mobile visitor would: confirm the player is visible without scrolling awkwardly, that tapping play actually starts music promptly, and that the control doesn't get hidden behind a mobile menu or theme element.

6. Rotate it back off after the season

A holiday playlist left running into February reads as an oversight, not a choice. Set a reminder alongside your BFCM setup to switch the store back to a general playlist — or turn background music off entirely — once the season ends, the same way you'd take down other seasonal storefront changes.

Frequently asked questions

Should background music autoplay during a big sale like BFCM?

No. Autoplay risk doesn't change because traffic is higher — if anything, a store under heavy traffic is a worse time to introduce unexpected audio for visitors using assistive technology. Keep playback visitor-initiated year-round.

How early should I turn on holiday music?

A few weeks before BFCM is enough time to test the playlist and player placement without rushing, and it avoids making storefront audio changes during your busiest sales days.

Do I need a different playlist for BFCM versus Christmas?

It's worth considering. BFCM is a sales-driven moment; Christmas is a seasonal mood. Many stores run a shorter, energetic playlist through the sale window and switch to a more traditional holiday rotation once December begins.
