Bulk and Corporate Gift Cards on Shopify: What Each Plan Actually Lets You Do

Short answer: Shopify's Gift Card API — the thing that lets a store programmatically issue many gift cards at once, for a company store or an employee gifting program — is gated to Shopify Plus. Standard-plan merchants can still issue gift cards in bulk, just manually, one at a time or through a CSV-based app, not through direct API access.

A confusion that shows up right when merchants need it least

Corporate and bulk gift carding usually comes up under time pressure — a company wants to send 50 or 200 gift cards to employees before a holiday deadline, or a client-gifting campaign needs to go out this week. That's exactly when merchants discover, often for the first time, that Shopify's own Gift Card API for programmatic bulk issuance is a Shopify Plus feature, and nobody explained that plan boundary anywhere they'd have seen it in advance.

What's actually gated, and what isn't

  • Gated to Shopify Plus: direct API access for programmatically creating gift cards in bulk — the kind of workflow that lets a developer or an app generate hundreds of unique codes in one automated batch.
  • Available on every plan: creating individual gift cards manually through the Shopify admin, and selling gift cards as a product through your storefront the normal way a customer would buy one.
  • Available on every plan, through apps: several third-party bulk gift card apps handle the batch-creation workflow — uploading a recipient list, generating a card per row — without requiring direct API access, because the app itself holds the necessary permissions.

What standard-plan merchants can actually do

If you're not on Shopify Plus and need to issue gift cards to a list of employees or clients, the practical path is a bulk gift-carding app built for this — most read a spreadsheet of names, emails, and amounts and generate one gift card per row, handling the batch creation the API gating would otherwise block you from doing yourself. That covers the purchase-and-issue side of a corporate gifting program on any plan.

What bulk-issuing apps generally don't cover

Batch-issuing tools are built to solve the distribution problem — getting a code to each recipient — not the follow-up question every corporate gifting program eventually runs into: how much of the batch has actually been redeemed, and can each recipient check their own balance without asking the company that bought the cards? That's a separate, self-service balance-check need, and it applies the same way whether the cards were issued manually, through an API on Shopify Plus, or through a bulk-issuing app on a standard plan — see our related guide on checking balances across bulk-purchased gift cards for that side of the problem.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro doesn't touch the issuing side of a corporate gifting program — that's a plan and API question that depends on your Shopify tier and whichever bulk-issuing app you use. What it adds is the piece that comes after issuance: a self-service balance check every recipient can use directly, regardless of which plan or app created their card in the first place.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Shopify Plus to run a corporate gifting program at all?

No. Plus is only required for direct programmatic API access to bulk-create gift cards yourself. A standard-plan store can still run a corporate gifting program using a bulk-issuing app or by creating cards manually for smaller batches.

Is there a workaround to bulk-issue without Plus or an app?

Not a practical one at scale — creating gift cards one at a time through the admin works for a handful of recipients, but doesn't reasonably scale to dozens or hundreds without either Plus-level API access or a purpose-built bulk-issuing app.

Does upgrading to Shopify Plus automatically give me a bulk-issuing tool?

No — Plus unlocks the API access a developer would need to build or connect a bulk-issuing workflow. It doesn't include a ready-made bulk gift card interface on its own.
