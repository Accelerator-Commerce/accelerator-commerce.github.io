Shopify Retired checkout.liquid and Scripts — Is Your Gift Card Balance Checker Still Working?

Short answer: If a gift-card or checkout tool you rely on was built on checkout.liquid or Shopify Scripts, its 2026 migration deadline has already passed. The fix isn't guesswork — test your own storefront right now, and ask any vendor a direct question about Checkout Extensibility support rather than assuming "it still shows up" means it still works correctly.

What actually changed, and when

Shopify spent 2026 forcing merchants off two older checkout customization methods: checkout.liquid, the old template-based way of editing the checkout page directly, and Shopify Scripts, the Plus-tier feature merchants used for custom discount, shipping, and payment logic at checkout. Coverage of the rollout cites deadlines ranging from June 30 to August 13, 2026 depending on the store and migration track, and all of them have now passed. On top of that, apps that relied on injecting script tags into checkout lost that ability entirely as of October 1, 2026, with full removal following on March 1, 2027.

The replacement is Checkout Extensibility: checkout customizations now run as sandboxed UI extensions built with Shopify's newer tooling, not as free-form code injected into a template. That's a real architecture change, not a rebrand — an app that worked by editing or injecting into the old checkout has to be rebuilt, not just updated, to keep working under the new model.

Why this matters specifically for a gift-card balance checker

Most gift-card balance-checker apps live on the storefront or account side — a dedicated page or widget where a shopper types in a code — not inside the checkout flow itself. But some older gift-card and checkout-customization apps did hook into checkout directly: a balance reminder injected via a checkout script tag, custom logic for combining multiple gift cards written as a Shopify Script, or a "redeem gift card" prompt added to the Thank You or order status page through the old template system. Any of that is exactly the kind of customization the 2026 deadlines targeted. Migration guides explicitly warn that some apps still on the Shopify App Store simply won't receive a Checkout Extensibility update at all, and merchants are told to ask each vendor directly rather than assume compatibility.

Test your storefront right now

  • Walk through a real checkout with a gift card applied. Add a product to cart, apply a gift card code at checkout, and confirm the balance and remaining-balance math still display correctly on the order confirmation and Thank You page.
  • Check whether anything that used to appear at checkout has quietly vanished. A missing balance reminder or a broken "apply another gift card" prompt is a classic sign of lost script-tag functionality — it won't throw an error, it will just stop showing up.
  • Look for a Checkout Extensibility notice in your Shopify admin. Shopify flags apps and customizations still using deprecated checkout methods directly in admin notifications — don't rely on memory of what you installed months ago.
  • Test combining multiple gift cards on one order if your store allows it — this is exactly the kind of logic merchants built with Shopify Scripts, and a quiet regression here is easy to miss until a customer reports it.

What to ask any gift-card app vendor

Don't ask "are you compatible with Checkout Extensibility?" — that's an easy yes to give without much behind it. Ask instead: does any part of your app's gift-card functionality run inside the checkout flow itself, and if so, has that specific piece been rebuilt as a Checkout UI extension, or was it removed? A vendor who can answer that precisely has actually done the migration work. One who can only point to a general compatibility statement may not have.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro's balance-checking widget runs on the storefront and account side through Shopify's supported app-embed approach — it was never built on checkout.liquid or Shopify Scripts, so none of the 2026 deprecation deadlines touch it. That's also a reason to be cautious before installing any checkout-adjacent app without checking its compatibility story first — see our guide to what to check before installing a gift card app for the broader pattern of questions worth asking before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Will Shopify just quietly fix this for me without any action on my part?

Not reliably. Shopify's platform migration covers its own checkout infrastructure, but whether a specific third-party app's feature gets rebuilt for Checkout Extensibility is up to that app's developer — some apps have already been migrated, and migration guides warn plainly that others won't be.

My gift card balance checker is on a dedicated page, not at checkout — does any of this affect me?

Probably not directly. A storefront or account-page balance checker that never touched checkout.liquid or Shopify Scripts isn't affected by these specific deadlines. The risk is narrower and checkout-specific — but it's still worth confirming with your vendor if you're unsure which approach your app uses.

What happens after the March 1, 2027 full-removal date?

Script tags injected into checkout stop working entirely, with no fallback. Anything still depending on that mechanism by then needs to have already moved to a Checkout UI extension, or it simply disappears from the checkout experience.
