The One POS Setting That Breaks Physical Gift Cards on Shopify

Quick answer: Shopify POS has a setting under Settings > Gift Cards > Issue digital or physical gift cards that defaults to digital-only. If staff can't sell a custom-amount physical gift card at the register, this is almost always why — flip it to include physical cards and the option reappears.

A setup trap that looks like a bug

If you've stocked physical gift cards, tried to ring one up at custom value on Shopify POS, and the option simply isn't there, the instinct is to assume something's broken — a theme issue, an app conflict, a POS glitch. It isn't. It's a single toggle in POS settings that defaults to digital-only gift cards, and nothing in Shopify's own onboarding flags it when you're setting up gift cards for the first time.

Where the setting actually lives

In the Shopify admin, go to Settings > Gift Cards. Look for the option to issue digital or physical gift cards — depending on your POS version this may be phrased as a toggle or a dropdown choosing which format staff can sell. If it's set to digital only, that's the entire explanation: POS will let staff sell pre-set digital gift card products, but the custom-amount physical card flow staff expect at the register won't appear until this is switched to include physical cards.

Why this trips up so many merchants

Most merchants think about gift cards as a product decision — do we stock physical cards, do we sell digital ones, do we do both — and don't expect a separate platform-level switch to gate that choice independently. Because the setting defaults to digital-only, any merchant who orders physical cards without knowing to check this first will hit a dead end at the register during a live sale, which is the worst possible time to discover a configuration gap.

A related trap: migrated card balances don't carry over automatically

If you're moving from another platform or a previous gift card program, know that Shopify POS only recognizes gift cards it issued itself — a physical card with a balance from a legacy system isn't something Shopify will honor out of the box. Merchants in this situation have had to use a separate bulk-import step just to bring existing card balances onto Shopify before POS will recognize them at all. Budget time for that migration step separately from the digital/physical toggle above — they're two different problems that happen to show up in the same part of the setup process.

Checklist: setting up physical gift cards on POS

  • Check Settings > Gift Cards and confirm physical gift cards are enabled, not just digital.
  • Test a custom-amount sale at the register before your physical cards go on display, not after a customer is already standing there with one.
  • If migrating legacy card balances, plan for a separate import step — Shopify won't recognize a balance it didn't issue.
  • Give staff a fast way to check a card's balance once it's sold, so a redemption doesn't turn into a manual admin lookup.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see the option to sell a custom-amount physical gift card on POS?

Check Settings > Gift Cards for the digital/physical issuance setting — it defaults to digital-only, which hides the physical, custom-amount option at the register until it's changed.

Does changing this setting affect gift cards I've already sold?

No — it only controls what staff can sell going forward. Cards already issued, digital or physical, keep working as they were.

Can I sell both digital and physical gift cards at the same time?

Yes, once the setting includes physical cards, staff can offer either format at the register — it's not an either/or choice for the store, just a gate that has to be opened first.
