Why Did My Shopify Audio Player Stop Working?

Short answer: An audio player that worked yesterday and doesn't today is almost always caused by one of three things: a theme update that changed or removed the markup the player relied on, a Shopify platform change that affected how app embeds load, or an app-embed setting that got toggled off. All three are fixable, and none of them mean the app itself is broken.

Theme updates are the most common cause

Updating a Shopify theme — even a minor version bump — can change template structure, remove a section the player embed depended on, or alter how theme app extensions are loaded. If a player disappeared right after a theme update, that's the first place to look: check whether the app embed is still enabled in the theme editor under App embeds, since theme updates can sometimes reset that toggle.

Shopify platform and API changes

Less often, a break traces back to Shopify itself. Audio and embed-style apps that depend on specific storefront APIs can be affected when Shopify ships changes to how those APIs behave — this has happened publicly to other audio-player apps, where a platform-side change broke playback until the app was updated to match. When a player breaks for every merchant using an app at the same time, rather than for one store after a theme change, a platform-level change is the more likely cause, and the fix has to come from the app's side, not the merchant's theme.

App-embed misconfiguration

A player embed is usually an opt-in toggle inside the theme editor, separate from installing the app itself. It's easy to lose track of that toggle — a theme switch, a duplicated theme used for testing, or a collaborator adjusting embeds can all turn it off without anyone noticing until a customer reports the player is gone.

A mobile-specific case worth knowing about

Audio playback issues don't always affect every device the same way. A reported case of an audio player working on desktop but not starting on mobile product pages is a good reminder to always test playback on an actual phone, not just a resized desktop browser — mobile browsers handle autoplay and user-interaction requirements for audio differently, and a fix that works on desktop doesn't always carry over.

A troubleshooting checklist

  • Check Theme editor → App embeds and confirm the player embed is still switched on.
  • Confirm the issue happens on the live theme, not just an unpublished draft theme.
  • Test on both desktop and mobile — a mobile-only failure points to a different cause than a site-wide one.
  • Check whether the issue started right after a theme update or theme change, which narrows the cause to theme markup rather than the app.
  • If the app is up to date and the embed is enabled, but the issue is happening broadly, it may be a platform-side change — contact the app's support rather than continuing to troubleshoot theme settings.

How Listen Now approaches reliability

Listen Now uses Shopify's theme app extension framework for its player embed rather than injecting markup directly into theme files, which keeps it more resilient to theme updates and gives merchants a single, visible on/off toggle in the theme editor to check first when something looks wrong.

Frequently asked questions

My player disappeared after I changed themes. Is that expected?

Yes — app embeds are enabled per theme, so switching to a new or duplicated theme requires re-enabling the player embed in that theme's editor.

How do I tell if it's my theme or the app?

If the issue affects only your store right after a theme change, suspect the theme. If it started for no local reason and affects playback broadly, suspect a platform-side change and reach out to the app's support.
