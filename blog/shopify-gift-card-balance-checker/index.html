Shopify Gift Card Balance Checker: What to Know

A gift card balance checker lets customers determine the remaining value of a Shopify gift card. The experience can be especially useful for stores that sell gift cards online or in person.

Why gift card balances matter

Gift cards often get spent across multiple orders. After a partial redemption, customers may not know what remains. A clear balance-checking workflow reduces friction and helps customers confidently use the rest of their gift card.

Gift cards and Shopify POS

Shopify POS includes native gift card functionality and can show remaining balances in relevant customer and gift-card workflows. Merchants can use an app when they want a more dedicated customer-facing experience.

Gift Check Pro

Gift Check Pro is built around checking gift card balances on Shopify. Its purpose is straightforward: give customers a convenient place to find their remaining balance.

Questions merchants should ask

  • Can customers find the balance checker easily?
  • Does it work well on mobile?
  • Does it fit the store's branding?
  • Can staff access the relevant information when serving customers?
