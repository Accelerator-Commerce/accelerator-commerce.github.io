Already run a podcast with an RSS feed? Import it straight into your store instead of uploading episodes one by one.

  1. Go to Settings > RSS Feed and click Configure.
  2. Paste in your feed URL.
  3. Listen Now! pulls in your episodes and adds them to your store's blog, one post per episode.

You can check on past imports and see logs any time from the RSS setup page.

Free plan: imports your 3 most recent episodes.
Starter and Pro plans: import your full feed.
