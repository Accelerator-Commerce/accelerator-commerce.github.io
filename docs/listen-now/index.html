Listen Now! adds sound to your Shopify store — no coding required.

Use it to:

  • Play background music or sound clips on a product page
  • Turn a blog post into something customers can listen to (text-to-speech)
  • Share podcast episodes or audiobook chapters right on your site

Upload a file, pick where it should play, and turn on the player. That's the whole process.

New here? Start with Quickstart.

Need help? Email us any time at help@acceleratorcommerce.com.
