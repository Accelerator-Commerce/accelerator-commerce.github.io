Running a Loyalty App and a Gift Card Balance Checker? Here's Why the Numbers Don't Match

Short answer: A loyalty app like Smile.io and a gift-card balance checker aren't reading from the same place. Shopify itself holds and displays store credit balances; a loyalty app only logs when that credit gets redeemed. Run both, and a customer can end up looking at two different numbers that are each correct for a different thing.

Two tools, two ledgers

A gift card is a self-contained record with its own code — Gift Check Pro and apps like it read that balance directly from Shopify's native gift-card system. A loyalty program's store credit is a different mechanism entirely: points get earned, converted to a dollar value, and issued as account-level credit. According to Smile.io's own help documentation, that store credit balance is actually held and displayed by Shopify, not by Smile — Smile's role is to log that a reward was redeemed, not to be the system of record for what a customer has left to spend. Two tools can both be technically correct and still show a customer two different "balances," because they're answering two different questions.

The legacy-account wrinkle that makes it worse

Smile's store-credit rewards are built to display correctly on Shopify's newer customer account system. A cited Smile app-store review describes exactly the failure mode this creates: a points balance that simply doesn't render on a legacy customer-account page. For a store that hasn't moved to new customer accounts, that's not a loyalty-app bug a merchant can fix from the loyalty app's side — it's an account-system mismatch that makes the loyalty balance effectively invisible to the shopper, even though the credit itself is still there.

What this looks like from a shopper's side

A customer who's earned loyalty rewards and also holds a gift card has no reason to know these are two separate systems. They check a gift-card balance page expecting it to reflect their loyalty credit too, or they see a points total in the loyalty widget and assume it's spendable the same way a gift card code is — forgetting that gift cards are redeemed with a code at checkout, while store credit from a loyalty program typically applies automatically once they're logged in. See our guide to telling a gift card and store credit apart for that underlying distinction — this is the specific way it shows up when a loyalty app is the source of the store credit side.

What merchants can actually do about it

  • Confirm you're on Shopify's new customer accounts. If store credit from a loyalty program isn't displaying for some customers, check the account system first — before assuming the loyalty app itself is broken.
  • Label each balance for what it actually is. "Gift card balance" and "loyalty store credit" are not interchangeable labels on a storefront, even if both spend like money at checkout. Vague wording like "your balance" invites exactly this confusion.
  • Point each tool at its own job. A gift-card balance checker should answer "what's left on this code," not attempt to also surface loyalty-program credit it was never built to read. Trying to merge the two into one number usually creates more confusion than it resolves.
  • Expect support tickets to name the wrong tool. A customer confused about a missing loyalty balance will often contact support about their "gift card" instead — train staff to ask which program the credit came from before troubleshooting.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro is built to answer one question clearly: what's left on a specific Shopify native gift card, read straight from Shopify's own ledger. It isn't designed to merge in balances from a separate loyalty platform's store-credit system, and that's intentional — see our guide on checking gift card balances across multiple issuing sources for the related, broader problem of trying to unify balances that live in genuinely different systems. The fix for the loyalty-vs-gift-card confusion isn't one tool pretending to cover both — it's clear labeling of which balance came from where.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make my loyalty app and my gift card balance checker show one combined number?

Not cleanly, and we wouldn't recommend trying. The two balances live in genuinely separate systems with different redemption mechanics — merging them into a single displayed figure tends to create new confusion (and new support tickets) rather than resolving the original one.

Is this a bug in Smile.io or in Shopify's gift card system?

Neither, strictly speaking — it's an architecture difference. Smile's own documentation describes this division of responsibility directly: Shopify holds and displays the store credit balance, and Smile logs the redemption event. The legacy-account display issue is a separate, specific bug pattern tied to which customer-account system a store is running.

Does this affect every loyalty app, or just Smile.io?

The underlying pattern — a loyalty platform issuing store credit that Shopify itself holds and displays — is common across loyalty apps built on Shopify's native store-credit feature, not unique to one vendor. Smile.io's documentation is simply the clearest public explanation of how that division works.
