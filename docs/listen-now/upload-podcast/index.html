Shopify's built-in file storage will hold your audio, but it won't play it for visitors — you'd need to build a player yourself. Listen Now! gives you that player, no coding involved.

Option 1: Upload episodes one at a time

Great for a new or small podcast:

  1. Upload each episode (MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, or M4A, up to 20 MB or 50 MB via the Upload Track wizard).
  2. Assign it to a blog post — most merchants create one post per episode.
  3. Turn on the player for that post.

Option 2: Import your whole feed

Already have an RSS feed? See RSS Feed to import your episodes automatically instead of uploading them one by one. (Free plan imports your 3 most recent episodes; Starter and Pro import the full feed.)
