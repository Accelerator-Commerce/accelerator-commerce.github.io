Why Shopify Won't Let You Default Refunds to a Gift Card

Short answer: Shopify always defaults a refund to the customer's original payment method. There's no admin setting to make "refund to gift card" the default for every return, even for merchants running a strict store-credit-only return policy. Staff have to manually select gift card on every single refund, which creates two real risks running in opposite directions: forgetting to switch it, and accidentally switching it when you shouldn't.

A real, unresolved request

A merchant running a store-credit-only return policy asked directly in the Shopify Community whether "refund to Gift Card" could be set as the default choice for every return, instead of Shopify's built-in default of refunding to the original payment method. The answer confirmed what the merchant suspected: Shopify doesn't support this natively, out of the box. Every single refund starts pre-selected to the original payment method, and a staff member has to actively change that selection to gift card each time — there's no store-wide policy setting that flips the default.

The two failure modes this creates

  • Forgetting to switch it. A store with a strict "returns are store credit only" policy is depending entirely on every staff member remembering, on every return, to manually change the refund destination away from Shopify's default. Miss it once, and a customer gets cash back on a return that was supposed to be store-credit-only — a policy violation and a real revenue leak if it happens repeatedly.
  • The opposite mistake: refunding to a gift card by accident. A separate, real Community thread shows the reverse failure — a merchant accidentally refunded to a gift card when they meant to refund the customer's original credit card. Because the refund screen presents payment-method options together, it's possible to select the wrong one under normal order-processing pressure, and the customer ends up with a gift card balance instead of money back on their card.

Both mistakes point to the same root cause: Shopify's refund screen treats "which destination" as a per-transaction manual choice with no store-level policy override, so the outcome depends entirely on an individual staff member getting it right, every time, with no safety net.

What merchants actually do about it

  • Build it into staff training and refund scripts explicitly. Since there's no system-level default to rely on, a written refund procedure that states "always select gift card, confirm before submitting" is the only real mitigation available natively.
  • Add a visible confirmation step before submitting a refund. Some stores add an internal checklist or a second-person confirmation for high-value refunds specifically to catch a wrong-destination selection before it's final.
  • Check the resulting gift card balance immediately after any refund. Whichever direction the mistake goes — cash back that should've been credit, or credit that should've been cash — the fastest way to catch it is confirming the actual outcome right after the refund is processed, rather than waiting for a customer to notice and report it.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro doesn't change how Shopify's refund screen defaults — that's a platform-level behavior no app can override. What it does is make the "did the refund actually land as a gift card, and for how much" question a fast lookup instead of a support ticket, for both staff double-checking a refund they just processed and customers checking a balance that unexpectedly changed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set a store-wide default so refunds always go to a gift card?

No. Shopify has no setting for this. Every refund defaults to the original payment method, and gift card has to be selected manually on each one.

What happens if a refund is accidentally sent to a gift card instead of the original card?

The customer ends up with a gift card balance instead of money back on their card. There's no automatic undo — a staff member would need to manually correct it, which may mean issuing a separate refund through the right channel and handling the gift card balance as a case-by-case exception.

Is this related to Shopify not letting me manually adjust a gift card's balance?

They're related but distinct limitations. This is about which payment method a refund defaults to; separately, Shopify also has no way to manually edit an already-issued gift card's balance. See our guide on gift card balance adjustments and refund reloads for that side of it.
