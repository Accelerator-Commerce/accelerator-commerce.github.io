Available on the Starter and Pro plans.

See how customers are actually listening. Go to Analytics in the main menu for:

  • Total plays — how many times your tracks have been played
  • Avg. completion — how much of a track people listen to, on average
  • Unique tracks played — how many different tracks got at least one play

Click into any track for its own breakdown, including plays, completion rate, and drop-off — where in the track listeners tend to stop. The same stats also show up on that track's Track Details page.

Analytics are collected straight from your storefront as customers listen, so numbers may take a short while to appear after a play happens.

Want the exact detail — what gets recorded and how each number is worked out? See How Analytics Works.
