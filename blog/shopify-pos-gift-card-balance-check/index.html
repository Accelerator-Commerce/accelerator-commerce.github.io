How to Check a Gift Card Balance on Shopify POS Without Starting a Sale

The problem: Shopify POS staff often can't look up a gift card balance without pulling up a full customer profile or starting a checkout just to see the number. That's slow, and starting a sale to check a balance risks an accidental charge.

Why this comes up so often

A customer walks up wanting to know what's left on a gift card before they decide what to buy. Staff should be able to answer that in seconds. But without a customer profile already pulled up, the fastest path many stores fall back on is scanning the card as if starting a sale, just to see the balance appear — then backing out of that sale.

Why the "start a sale" workaround is risky

  • A card gets scanned into an in-progress sale that a busy staff member can forget to cancel.
  • Line-busy moments make it easy to accidentally complete a $0 or partial checkout.
  • New or seasonal staff aren't always confident backing out of a started sale correctly.
  • It trains staff to treat a balance check as a mini-transaction instead of a quick lookup.

What a dedicated balance lookup should look like

Staff should be able to scan or enter a gift card code and get a balance back immediately, without that action ever touching a cart, a customer record, or the register total. It's a read-only question, and the tool used to answer it should be read-only too.

How Gift Check Pro helps at the counter

Gift Check Pro is built around checking gift card balances directly, separate from the checkout flow. Staff can look up a balance for a customer standing at the counter without opening a cart or requiring a full customer profile lookup first, and without any risk of that lookup turning into an accidental sale.

Setting up a faster in-store lookup

  • Make sure staff know the balance-check flow exists and where to find it — don't leave the checkout-scan workaround as the only trained method.
  • Keep the lookup accessible from the counter, not buried behind an admin-only screen.
  • Train new staff on the balance-check step before they're trained on backing out of a started sale.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shopify POS support gift card balances natively at all?

Shopify POS can surface gift card information tied to a customer profile or an in-progress sale, but that's not the same as a quick, standalone balance lookup a staff member can use before a customer has decided to buy anything.

Is this only a problem for busy stores?

It shows up everywhere, but it's most costly during busy periods, when staff are moving fast and least likely to double back and confirm a started sale was properly canceled.
