How to Let Customers Check a Gift Card Balance on Shopify

Why add a balance checker? Customers who received or partially used a gift card may want to know how much remains before shopping. A dedicated balance-checking experience makes that task easier to find.

How gift cards work in Shopify

Shopify supports gift cards as a payment method, and Shopify POS can display gift card information including remaining balance and transaction history for a customer.

Why merchants may want a storefront balance page

A storefront balance checker gives customers a self-service destination instead of requiring them to contact the merchant or start a checkout simply to determine the remaining value.

Gift Check Pro

Gift Check Pro is a Shopify app focused on gift card balance checking. It provides a customer-facing way to check gift card information and can also support POS-oriented workflows.

Good gift card UX

  • Make the balance checker easy to find.
  • Explain what information the customer needs.
  • Make the form usable on mobile.
  • Provide a clear result and next step.
  • Keep support information nearby if a balance cannot be found.
