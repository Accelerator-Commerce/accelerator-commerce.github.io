Listen Now! lets you add an audio player to your Shopify store. There are two ways to give customers something to listen to:

  1. Upload your own audio — music, a podcast episode, a voiceover, whatever you have as an MP3 or WAV file.
  2. Text-to-speech — Listen Now! reads your blog posts or product descriptions out loud automatically, so you don't need a recording at all.

Getting started

After you install the app from the Shopify App Store, you'll be asked to pick a plan (see Pricing Plans). If your store is a development or Partner store, you're dropped straight into a free developer mode instead — no plan needed while you're testing.

From there, the Dashboard walks you through a short setup checklist: add the player to your theme, upload a track, assign it to a page, and preview it live.

Ready to dive in? Head to Quickstart.
