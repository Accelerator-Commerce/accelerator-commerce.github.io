Go to My Account to see:

  • Store Details — your store name and contact email
  • Plan Details — your current plan and what it includes
  • Billing Details — how you're billed

Billing

You're billed through Shopify, alongside your other app charges — Listen Now! doesn't charge your card directly.

Cancelling

To cancel, uninstall the app from your Shopify Admin. There's no separate cancel button inside Listen Now!
