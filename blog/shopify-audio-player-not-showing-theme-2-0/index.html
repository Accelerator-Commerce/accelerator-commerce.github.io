Why Isn't My Audio Player Showing Up on My Shopify 2.0 Theme?

Short answer: On an Online Store 2.0 theme, installing an audio app doesn't automatically make the player appear. Most audio and widget apps ship as an app embed, which is off by default and has to be switched on separately, in Theme Settings → App embeds. If the player has never shown up at all, that toggle is the first thing to check.

Two different problems that look the same

"My audio player isn't working" usually means one of two very different things. Either it worked before and stopped — often caused by a theme update or a setting that got reset — or it never appeared in the first place after installing the app. This post is about the second case: a player that's never shown up, on a theme that's already Online Store 2.0. If you're dealing with a player that broke after previously working, that's a different set of causes.

What an app embed actually is

Online Store 2.0 introduced app embeds as the standard way apps add visible elements — buttons, players, badges, popups — to a storefront without editing theme code directly. An app embed is registered by the app but has to be turned on by the merchant, in the theme editor, per theme. That's a deliberate design: it means switching themes, or apps, doesn't silently inject code everywhere, but it also means an app can be fully installed and still be invisible until the embed is enabled.

Why this trips people up

Installing an app from the Shopify App Store feels like it should be the whole job — click install, and the feature appears. For app embeds, installing the app only makes the embed available; it doesn't turn it on. That extra step is easy to miss, especially if a merchant installs an app, previews the storefront, sees nothing, and assumes the install failed rather than that one toggle is off.

How to turn on an audio player app embed

  • In the Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on your live (published) theme — not a draft or duplicate theme, since embeds are enabled per theme.
  • Open App embeds in the left-hand theme editor panel (usually a small icon near the bottom of the block list).
  • Find the audio app in the list and switch its embed on.
  • Save, then check the live storefront — not just the theme editor preview.

Old code-injection players vs. app embeds

Before Online Store 2.0, some audio and widget apps worked by injecting a snippet directly into theme.liquid or another theme file. That approach doesn't need a toggle — it's just there once installed — but it's also more fragile: a theme update can overwrite or remove the injected code without warning. App embeds trade that "just there" simplicity for a single, visible on/off switch that survives theme updates, at the cost of one extra step to turn it on.

Why "works with all themes" is being marketed so heavily

Theme and app-embed compatibility has become an active selling point for audio and widget apps — several competitors specifically advertise working across Online Store 2.0 themes. That's a real distinction worth understanding rather than taking on faith: the meaningful question isn't just "does it work with my theme," but "is it a real app embed I can toggle in one place, or code injected somewhere I can't see."

How Listen Now handles this

Listen Now ships as a proper Online Store 2.0 app embed with a single toggle in Theme Settings → App embeds, rather than injecting code into theme files. If the player isn't appearing after install, that toggle — checked on the live, published theme — is the first and usually only place to look.

Frequently asked questions

I turned the embed on but still don't see the player. What now?

Confirm you're editing the theme that's actually published live, not a draft or duplicate — app embeds are per-theme, so enabling one on a draft theme won't show up for real visitors. Also check that you saved the theme editor changes, not just previewed them.

Is my theme too old to support app embeds?

App embeds require an Online Store 2.0 theme. If your theme predates that (an older "vintage" theme), app embeds won't be available at all, and the app will need a different installation method or theme update.
