How to Publish Tour Dates on Shopify

Best approach: Give every event a clear date, location and title, then publish upcoming appearances in a mobile-friendly calendar or list that customers can scan quickly.

Why put tour dates on your Shopify store?

If your business sells products and also appears in person, your Shopify store can become the central place customers discover where you'll be next.

What should an event listing include?

  • Event name
  • Date and time
  • Venue or location
  • City and region
  • Relevant ticket or registration link
  • Short description

Why use Fresh Cal?

Fresh Cal is built for publishing tour dates, live performances, speaking engagements, workshops, pop-ups, classes and other appearances in a clean, mobile-friendly list or calendar.

What about event SEO?

Structured event data can help search engines understand event details. Fresh Cal's Shopify App Store listing describes SEO-ready event schema markup and event-oriented publishing features.
