Can You Change a Shopify Gift Card's Delivery Date After Scheduling It?

Short answer: No. Shopify lets you schedule a gift card to be emailed to a recipient on a future date — useful for birthdays, anniversaries and holidays — but once that date is set, it can't be edited. The only native workaround is to cancel the scheduled card and issue a new one with the corrected date.

The feature works, until you need to change it

Scheduling a gift card for future delivery is a genuinely useful native Shopify feature: a merchant (or a customer buying a gift card as a present) can set a delivery date so the recipient gets the email on the day that actually matters, rather than the day it was purchased. The problem shows up after the fact — a merchant realizes the date is wrong, a customer asks for it to move by a day, or a birthday gift needs to shift because of a shipping delay elsewhere in the order. At that point, there's no edit option. The scheduled date is locked in from the moment it's set.

What merchants have tried instead

This isn't a hypothetical edge case — it's a recurring question, and merchants have approached it a few different ways:

  • Third-party gift card apps. Some merchants have tried apps built specifically around gift card workflows, expecting scheduling flexibility to be part of the package. In practice, several of these apps don't integrate cleanly with Shopify's own native gift card product, which creates a different problem: two gift card systems that don't agree with each other.
  • Giving up on scheduling entirely. At least one merchant abandoned a scheduled bonus-gift-card workflow altogether after testing multiple apps, falling back to discount codes with activation windows instead — a workaround that solves the timing problem but isn't really a gift card anymore.
  • Cancel and reissue. The most direct native fix: cancel the incorrectly scheduled gift card before its delivery date arrives, then issue a new one with the correct date. It works, but it means redoing the whole gift card, not just adjusting one field.

The cancel-and-reissue workaround, step by step

  1. Find the scheduled gift card in Shopify admin before its delivery date passes — once it's sent, there's nothing left to cancel.
  2. Deactivate or cancel the existing card so it won't be emailed on the original date.
  3. Issue a new gift card with the same value, recipient details and message, this time with the corrected delivery date.
  4. Confirm the new scheduled date before moving on — there's no second edit available if it's wrong again.

The catch is timing: this only works if the mistake is caught before the original delivery date. Once a scheduled card has actually been sent, canceling it doesn't un-send the email — the recipient has already received a card with the original balance and code.

Why this connects to balance checking later

A scheduled gift card, once it finally arrives, becomes exactly the same as any other gift card: a code with a balance attached. The scheduling problem is a delivery-timing issue that happens before the card is ever used — it has no effect on how the balance works or gets checked once the recipient actually has the card in hand. If a merchant reissues a card to fix a delivery date, it's worth double-checking that the original (canceled) card's code no longer shows a balance when checked, so a recipient can't accidentally end up with two working codes for the same gift.

Where Gift Check Pro fits

Gift Check Pro doesn't change how gift card scheduling or delivery works — that's a native Shopify feature with the limitation described above. What it does is give the eventual recipient (and merchant staff) a fast, self-serve way to confirm a card's balance once it's been delivered and used, including confirming that a canceled-and-reissued card's old code is no longer active.

Frequently asked questions

Can I schedule a gift card delivery date more than once for the same card?

No. A card's scheduled delivery date is set once and can't be modified. Changing the date requires canceling the existing card and creating a new one.

What happens if I cancel a scheduled gift card after it's already been sent?

Canceling it can stop the balance from being usable going forward, but it doesn't undo the email delivery itself — the recipient will have already seen a card with the original details.

Do third-party gift card apps solve the reschedule problem?

Results vary. Some apps that aren't built to integrate with Shopify's native gift card product can end up creating a second, disconnected system rather than actually fixing the scheduling limitation — worth testing carefully before relying on one for this specific use case.
