Shopify Plus: What Merchants Should Know

Quick answer: Shopify Plus is Shopify's enterprise offering for merchants with more complex scale, operational and customization requirements. The right choice depends on your business needs, not simply revenue.

Shopify Plus is designed for organizations that need more advanced capabilities around scale, international operations, automation, organization management and enterprise workflows.

What is Shopify Plus?

Shopify Plus builds on Shopify's core commerce platform while adding capabilities aimed at larger and more complex businesses. It is most useful when operational requirements have outgrown a simpler storefront setup.

Shopify vs. Shopify Plus

The key difference is not that one is a completely different ecommerce platform. Shopify Plus is an enterprise tier with additional capabilities, controls and support designed for more complex organizations.

When should you consider Shopify Plus?

  • You operate multiple markets or stores.
  • Your organization needs advanced automation.
  • You have complex user, permission or operational requirements.
  • You need enterprise-oriented support and capabilities.

Do Shopify apps work with Shopify Plus?

Many apps can be used by Shopify Plus merchants, but compatibility depends on the app and the specific feature being used. Always check the current app listing and documentation.
