Why Shopify Doesn't Have a "Check Gift Card Balance" Button (And What Actually Works)

Quick answer: Shopify has no native storefront button for shoppers to check a gift card balance, and no Storefront API method for developers to build one either. Merchants and staff are left with an admin lookup or an order-number search — not something you can hand a customer or put on your storefront. A dedicated balance-check app is what fills that specific, structural gap.

This isn't a one-off complaint — it's a confirmed gap

Search "check my Shopify gift card balance" and you'll find plenty of third-party guides walking through workarounds, but rarely an explanation of why a workaround is needed in the first place. Three independent, mutually-corroborating reports — from a shopper, a developer, and a merchant — confirm this is a real structural limitation of the platform, not a support fluke.

What shoppers run into

A brick-and-mortar retailer put it plainly in a long-running Shopify Community thread: "there is no easy way to do this. There is no 'check balance' button." The workaround described in that thread involves adding a fake $10,000 product to checkout just to see how much of it a gift card would cover — an absurd amount of friction for something that should be a one-field lookup. Shopify's own reply in the thread points merchants back to an admin-based or order-number lookup, not a shopper-facing tool. Over a year later, a commenter in the same thread noted plainly that nothing had changed.

What developers run into

The gap isn't a front-end oversight either — it goes down to the API level. Two separate developer threads confirm there is no native Storefront API method to query a gift card's balance directly. The only workaround developers have found is creating a throwaway checkout, applying the gift card to it, and reading the resulting AppliedGiftCard object to back-calculate what the real balance must have been. That's a real technique people are actually shipping to production because the direct method doesn't exist.

What merchants and staff run into

Without a purpose-built tool, staff end up doing one of two things: digging through the admin to find the gift card record and read its balance manually, or looking it up by order number if they happen to have it. Neither is something you can point a customer to directly, and neither scales to a checkout line or a phone call where someone just wants to know what's left on their card.

Why Shopify hasn't just added a button

Gift cards on Shopify are treated as a sensitive financial primitive rather than a generic storefront feature — the platform exposes gift card codes and balances carefully, similar to how it handles discount codes and payment details, rather than as an open, queryable object like a product. That's a reasonable security posture, but it also means the platform stops short of shipping the customer-facing lookup experience shoppers actually expect, leaving that layer to apps built specifically for it.

What actually works: a dedicated balance-check tool

The fix isn't a Shopify setting to toggle on — it's a purpose-built app that sits between the storefront (or POS) and the gift card data, doing the lookup safely without exposing raw admin access or requiring a throwaway checkout. Gift Check Pro gives shoppers a real, on-site "check your balance" field and gives staff the same fast lookup at the counter, closing exactly the gap described in all three threads above — no fake product, no manual admin digging, no back-calculated checkout trick required. Frequently asked questions

Is there really no way to check a gift card balance on Shopify at all?

There are workarounds — an admin lookup for staff, an order-number search, or a throwaway checkout trick for developers — but none of them are a real, shopper-facing "check balance" button, and none of them are things you'd want to expose directly to a customer.

Will Shopify eventually add this natively?

There's no indication it's planned. The Community thread describing the gap has been open for well over a year with no native fix shipped, which is part of why dedicated balance-check apps exist as a category.

Is a third-party balance-check tool safe to use for this?

Keeping the lookup on your own store's domain, through an app built for exactly this purpose, is safer than the alternative of customers searching for unaffiliated third-party checker sites. See our guide to avoiding gift card balance-check scams for more on why that distinction matters.
