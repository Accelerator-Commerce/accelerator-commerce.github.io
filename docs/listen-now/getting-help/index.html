Support
Contact support

Email us: help@acceleratorcommerce.com — a real person will get back to you.

In the app: open the main menu and choose Contact Us & Feedback under Get Help. It sends us a message along with some basic details about your store, so we can help faster.

Leave a review: if Listen Now! is working well for you, choosing Review App from the same menu takes you to Shopify's review prompt — it helps other merchants find us.
