Gift Check Pro vs. GCP Gift Card Balance: Comparing Shopify's Balance-Check Apps

Short answer: Gift Check Pro and GCP - Gift Card Balance both focus narrowly on the same job — letting merchants and customers check a Shopify gift card balance at POS and on the storefront, without the loyalty, cashback or store-credit features that bloat most gift card apps. The two are unusually close in positioning, so the real differences worth checking come down to setup, customer-facing polish and support, not the headline feature list.

Why these two get compared

Most "gift card app" round-ups are dominated by loyalty-and-store-credit platforms — think branded wallets, cashback and rewards points bundled in alongside gift cards. Gift Check Pro was built around a narrower, plainer job: a fast, reliable way to check a balance, for both POS and storefront. GCP - Gift Card Balance is one of the few other Shopify apps built around that exact same narrow job, with a similar feature description, which makes it the more relevant comparison than the loyalty-platform alternatives merchants usually find first.

What both apps share

  • A dedicated gift card balance check, not a side feature bolted onto a loyalty or store-credit product.
  • Support for checking a balance in both POS and storefront contexts.
  • No requirement that a merchant adopt a broader loyalty or rewards program just to get balance checking.

What to actually compare when the feature lists look identical

When two apps describe themselves almost the same way, the feature-list comparison stops being useful and the real decision comes down to things a screenshot doesn't show:

  • Setup time. How much theme or checkout configuration is needed before the balance checker is live, and how much of that is guided versus manual.
  • Customer-facing polish. Whether the balance-check page matches store branding out of the box, or looks like an obviously bolted-on widget.
  • Support responsiveness. For a utility app that touches checkout-adjacent flows, how quickly a merchant can get help if something breaks matters more than an extra feature neither app really needs.
  • Pricing as usage scales. Whether cost stays predictable as gift card volume grows, since that's where narrowly-scoped apps sometimes diverge even when day-one pricing looks similar.

Gift Check Pro's approach

Gift Check Pro keeps its scope deliberately narrow — balance checking at POS and on the storefront, nothing else — and treats that focus as the differentiator rather than a limitation. Merchants deciding between apps this closely matched should evaluate based on the setup and support experience directly, since the core promise is effectively the same on paper.
