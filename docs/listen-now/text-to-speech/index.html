Don't have a recording? Listen Now! can read your content out loud for you.

Blog posts

Turn on the Text-to-Speech widget on a blog article template, and it will automatically read the post's title and body. You can customize:

  • Look — "Classic" or "Modern" style, plus your own colors for background, buttons, and text
  • Voice — choose the language and voice
  • Speed — adjust how fast it speaks
  • Author name — optionally have it announce who wrote the post

Product pages

On product pages, the Text-to-Speech widget plays a single audio file using your browser's built-in audio player, so it works everywhere without extra plugins.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Settings > Text To Speech and click Enable (or start from Upload Track and choose text-to-speech as your source).
  2. This opens the Theme Editor to the right template.
  3. Drag the widget into place and adjust the voice, speed, and styling.

As with the regular audio player, the widget only appears once it has content to play — an empty widget stays hidden.
