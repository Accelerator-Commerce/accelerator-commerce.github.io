Shopify Apps for Accessibility: Where Apps Can Help

Apps can help, but they are not a complete accessibility strategy. Accessibility starts with a well-structured, keyboard-friendly, readable storefront. Apps can add specific experiences such as audio or text-to-speech.

Accessibility is broader than an app

Shopify's own accessibility guidance covers areas including keyboard and gesture controls, page structure, media, color and contrast, dynamic components and touch interactions. Merchants should treat app-based features as additions to good storefront fundamentals.

Where audio can help

Text-to-speech can give visitors another way to consume written content. It can be particularly useful for long articles, guides and educational material.

Using Listen Now

Listen Now can add an audio player to Shopify content. The written content should remain available, while the audio option gives visitors a choice in how they consume it.

Accessibility checklist for Shopify content

  • Use descriptive headings.
  • Keep text readable and well structured.
  • Ensure controls work with a keyboard.
  • Provide clear labels and instructions.
  • Do not autoplay audio unexpectedly.
  • Test on mobile and with assistive technology.
