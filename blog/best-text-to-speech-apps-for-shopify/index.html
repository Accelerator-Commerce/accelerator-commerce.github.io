Best Text-to-Speech Apps for Shopify Stores

What should you look for? A good Shopify text-to-speech app should make audio easy to add, give merchants control over where it appears, work well on mobile, and provide a clear listening experience without replacing the original text.

What is a Shopify text-to-speech app?

A Shopify text-to-speech app converts written store content into spoken audio so customers can listen instead of—or in addition to—reading. Common use cases include blog posts, educational pages, product information and long-form content.

Key features to compare

  • Support for the content types you actually publish.
  • Simple merchant setup.
  • Customizable player presentation.
  • Mobile-friendly controls.
  • Accessible play, pause and volume controls.
  • Reasonable performance and minimal storefront overhead.

Is text-to-speech good for ecommerce accessibility?

It can be a useful part of an accessible content strategy. It should complement semantic HTML, keyboard accessibility, readable typography, color contrast and other accessibility practices.

When should a merchant add text-to-speech?

It is especially useful when your store publishes substantial written content or when customers benefit from consuming information while doing something else.
