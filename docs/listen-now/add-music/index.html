Shopify lets you upload audio files under Content > Files, but on its own it won't play them for customers — you'd need to build your own player. That's a lot of coding for something that should be simple.

Listen Now! handles the whole thing:

  1. Upload your music file (MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, or M4A — up to 20 MB, or 50 MB through the Upload Track wizard).
  2. Choose where it should play — your homepage, a specific product, a page, or site-wide.
  3. Turn on the player in your Theme Editor. No code required.

That's it — your music is live on your store. See Quickstart for the full walkthrough.
