How to Add an Audio Player to Shopify Blog Posts

The simplest approach: Keep your Shopify blog post as normal HTML and add a dedicated audio experience alongside it. This lets visitors choose reading or listening.

Why put an audio player on a blog?

Long-form articles, tutorials and educational posts can be easier to consume when customers have the option to listen. Audio also gives your content another format without requiring you to create a separate podcast episode.

What makes a good blog audio player?

It should be obvious, compact and easy to control. Visitors should know what is playing and be able to pause or resume without hunting for controls.

Should the audio replace the article?

No. Keep the article text available. Text remains valuable for search, scanning, accessibility and visitors who prefer reading.

Using Listen Now

Listen Now is designed to add audio experiences to Shopify content. Use the app to manage audio and present a player where it makes sense for your storefront.
